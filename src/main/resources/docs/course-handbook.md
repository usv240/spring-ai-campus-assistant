# Northfield Institute of Technology - Student Handbook (sample data for the demo)

This handbook is fictional. It exists only so the RAG demo has private data
the model has never seen.

## Attendance
Students must maintain a minimum of 82% attendance in each course to be
eligible for the final assessment. Students between 75% and 82% may apply to
the Dean of Academics for condonation with a medical certificate. Below 75%,
the student must re-register for the course in the next semester.

## Grading
Continuous assessment carries 60% of the final grade: two quizzes (10% each),
one mini project (20%) and a lab record (20%). The final assessment carries 40%.
A minimum of 40 out of 100 overall is required to pass. Grade S is 90 and above,
A is 80-89, B is 70-79, C is 60-69, D is 50-59, E is 40-49 and F is below 40.

## Re-evaluation
Students may request re-evaluation of the final assessment within 5 working
days of results being published. The fee is 750 rupees per course and is
refunded if the grade changes.

## Labs
Labs are open from 9 AM to 6 PM on weekdays. The AI Lab has 40 GPU workstations,
the Cloud Lab offers sandbox accounts on two cloud providers, and the IoT Lab has
sensor kits that can be borrowed for up to 7 days. Slots must be booked through
the Campus Assistant or the lab portal at least 2 hours in advance.

## Library
Students may borrow up to 6 books for 14 days. The late fee is 5 rupees per book
per day. The digital library gives access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library and
O'Reilly Learning using the student's institute login.

## Hackathons and clubs
The Developer Club runs a 24-hour hackathon every semester. Teams have 2 to 4
members. Winning teams receive 2 extra credits under the experiential learning
category, once per academic year.

## Internships
Students may take a full-semester internship in their final year if their CGPA is
7.5 or above and they have no pending backlogs. The faculty mentor must approve
the internship offer letter before the student joins.
